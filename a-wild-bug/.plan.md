# A Wild Bug

> **Vertical Slice Plan** — locked 2026-09-12
> Tagline: *One tiny harvest. One huge day.*

An original, bug's-eye-view action-collecting game built with Three.js, inspired by the premise of
*A Bug's Life* (harvest quota under threat from grasshoppers) and the public-domain fable
*The Ant and the Grasshopper*. All characters and names are original — this is not a Disney/Pixar
product and must not use their characters or trademarks.

You are **Pip**, a small inventor ant. Each day, Hopper's gang demands a grain quota by sunset.
Explore a giant meadow, harvest grain, grow spring-plants from seeds to reach impossible places,
and dodge grasshopper patrols — deliver the quota before the light dies.

---

## 1. Core loop (what the slice must prove is fun)

```
Explore the meadow
   → harvest grain (carry 1, stack upgrades)
   → risk: grasshopper patrols steal your carried grain
   → use seed-plants to reach rich patches
   → deposit at the anthill
   → meet quota before sunset (the sinking sun IS the timer)
   → day complete, small upgrade unlocked
   → next day
```

A full slice run is **5–8 minutes**: one in-game "day" with a real win condition and a real lose
condition.

## 2. The slice: "Day One on Clover Hill"

One stylized meadow diorama at ant scale: towering grass blades, clover, a dew-dropped mushroom
grove, a fallen apple as a landmark, the anthill mound as home base, and a day-to-sunset lighting
cycle. Character, enemy, HUD, audio, menus all present — one level, everything final-quality.

## 3. Feature scope

| In the slice | Deferred to post-slice |
|---|---|
| Third-person controller: walk, run, jump, carry/throw | Climbing vertical grass, swimming, flying |
| Grain harvest, carry, deposit + quota HUD (grain count, sun timer) | Multiple days/levels, economy, upgrades beyond one |
| Seed mechanic v1: jump on a seed → spring plant launches you to high ledges | Seed tiers, berry-shooting plants, uprooting |
| One enemy: patrolling grasshopper (patrol → spot → chase → steal) with stun-throw counter | Boss fights (Thumper-style, the bird, Hopper), other enemy types |
| Health leaf (3 bites), hit feedback, steal-and-recover tension | Lives/game-over meta, checkpoints |
| Title screen, pause, win screen ("Day 2" tease), lose screen | Save system, settings, accessibility options |
| Audio: music loop, footsteps, pickup/throw, grasshopper sting | Voice, dynamic score |

## 4. Tech stack

- **Vite + TypeScript + Three.js** (vanilla — no React/R3F, minimal deps)
- **Custom kinematic character controller** (capsule vs. terrain heightfield + simple prop
  colliders) — no physics engine for the slice
- **InstancedMesh grass** in wind-blown clumps, wind in the vertex shader, fog for draw distance
  (clump quads, not single blades — overdraw is the killer)
- **Assets:** low-poly Blender models or fully procedural; the ant gets a six-leg sine-offset
  procedural walk cycle (zero rigging cost)

**Definition of done:** someone who has never seen the project clicks a link and, within 5–8

and a reason to want "Day 2." Every system they touch is the real system, not a placeholder.

## 6. Risks

1. **Movement feel** — the make-or-break gate is M1; do not build M2+ content on feel that isn't fun yet.
2. **Grass overdraw** on weak GPUs — clump quads, not blades; keep blade vertex counts low.
3. **Seed-mechanic scope creep** — v1 is *one* plant type, period.

## 7. Naming decision log

Pattern explored: "A ___'s Life" (Pixar-adjacent), bug-species variants (A Pillbug's/Firefly's/
Ladybug's Life), and ant-true variants (A Forager's Life, A Worker's Life). **Locked: "A Wild Bug"**
— keeps the storybook "A ___'s Life" energy, stays generic-bug so the hero cast can grow
(ants, pillbugs, fireflies), and doesn't collide head-on with the Disney/Pixar trademark.
